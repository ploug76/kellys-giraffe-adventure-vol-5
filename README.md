# Kelly's Giraffe Adventure Vol. 5: Midnight Zoo Escape

Kelly's giraffe sneaks through the zoo at midnight! Free Ellie the Elephant, Percy the Penguin and Kiki the Koala, then use their help (water spray, belly slides under gates, pole climbing to switches) across 9 moonlit levels and 4 silly zookeeper-robot bosses to rescue Peter and the birthday cake from the gift shop.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/kellys-giraffe-adventure-vol-5/

Controls: swipe and hold anywhere to move. JUMP (orange) jumps. ACTION (green) calls your current friend's help, and also does the giraffe moves: head bonk, neck ram (double-tap), tongue, ring swing, neck grab (hold UP at a ledge) and neck pound (DOWN+ACTION in the air). Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Z/Space jump, X action, C ram, P pause, M sound. Controllers: stick/D-pad move, A jump, X/B action, Y ram, Start pause.
